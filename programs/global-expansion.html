The Global Artist Expansion™

One Sound. Multiple Markets. Worldwide Possibilities.

Investment: $20,000–$30,000+
APPLY FOR GLOBAL ARTIST EXPANSIONRequest a Consultation

Your music has no borders. Your audience shouldn’t either.

The Global Artist Expansion™ is our premium international artist development and promotional experience.

Designed for artists interested in expanding beyond their immediate markets, this program focuses on international artist positioning, cross-market promotional strategy, global media outreach, and international industry-resource development.

We help artists explore new audiences, identify potential markets, and develop strategies for introducing their music to the international entertainment marketplace.

Who should apply?

Established and emerging recording artists who have a clear musical identity, an existing music catalog, and a genuine interest in expanding their professional reach into additional countries and international markets.
