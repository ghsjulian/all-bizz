Application Review & Enrollment

What Happens After You Apply?

1

Step 1 – Application Received

Your application and submitted materials are received for consideration.

2

Step 2 – Artist Evaluation

Our team reviews your musical identity, career objectives, promotional needs, and program eligibility.

3

Step 3 – Private Consultation

Qualified applicants may be invited to schedule a private consultation with an All Bizz Entertainment representative.

4

Step 4 – Program Recommendation

Following consultation, eligible applicants may receive a recommended service package and customized proposal outlining deliverables, pricing, campaign objectives, and estimated timelines.

5

Step 5 – Enrollment & Agreement

Approved artists receive an enrollment agreement detailing the scope of services, payment arrangements, responsibilities, and applicable policies.

6

Step 6 – Program Activation

Once enrollment requirements are satisfied, onboarding begins and the agreed-upon services are activated.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance or enrollment. Read important program information.
